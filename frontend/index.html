Important scientific boundary

Model-backed inference is opt-in and depends on configured checkpoints, optional dependencies, hardware and model licences. Current pipelines include YOLO detection/tracking, Transformers classification/NER, optional Tesseract OCR, faster-whisper transcription and pyannote diarization. No generic activity-recognition or universal segmentation guarantee is made. Calibration and fairness reports require independent gold data and explicit subgroup labels. Outputs remain proposals until human review.
